DIRECTION A · THE LIT MODEL

An axonometric model with a finite edge, not an infinite map. Every depth cue the current flat UI lacks is doing work here: occlusion, contact shadow, cast light, a strict scale hierarchy. The world is dark except where a lease is held — two pools of light in a quiet campus is what 'two agents are working' looks like, and an empty intake yard is what 'nothing has been noticed' looks like.
